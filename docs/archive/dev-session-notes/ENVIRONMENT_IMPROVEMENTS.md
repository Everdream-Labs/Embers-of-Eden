# 🌲 ENVIRONMENT IMPROVEMENTS - EMBERS OF EDEN

## ✅ Enhanced Game View & Environment

### 📷 **Camera Zoom Updated**
- **Previous**: 1.5x zoom
- **New**: 3.0x zoom (doubled)
- **Result**: Player appears **twice as big** and more detailed
- **Benefits**: Better visibility of your armored character, more intimate gameplay feel

### 🌲 **Improved Tree Distribution**
- **Previous**: 25 randomly scattered trees
- **New**: 6 clusters with 2-6 trees each
- **Features**:
  - **Patchy distribution** - trees grouped naturally
  - **Sparse coverage** - more open spaces for movement
  - **Cluster variation** - different sizes and spacing
  - **Natural look** - trees scattered around cluster centers

### 🪨 **New Rock Clusters**
- **Added**: 8 rock clusters throughout the level
- **Each cluster**: 3-8 rocks with collision
- **Features**:
  - **Variable sizes** - rocks range from 32-48 pixels
  - **Realistic collision** - circular collision shapes
  - **Color variety** - gray, brown-gray, blue-gray variations
  - **Natural placement** - scattered around cluster centers
  - **Solid obstacles** - player must navigate around them

### 🌸 **Enhanced Decorations**
- **Flower patches**: 12 scattered decorative flowers
- **No collision** - purely visual elements
- **Background layer** - don't interfere with gameplay
