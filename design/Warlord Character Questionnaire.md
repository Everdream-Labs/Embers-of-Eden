# Embers of Eden — Warlord Character Questionnaire
**Purpose:** lock a name, a face and a personality for every Warlord, so the next step is an image prompt. Viktor and Ayla are the style masters. Sprites come later. Profiles first.
**Companion to:** the RTS-era `Scope Questionnaire.md` (now archived in `design/Reference/`). Mechanics stay there (traits, signatures, vehicles, animals, chassis, flag construction, whether we record voices). This file is the people.
**Compiled:** 1 November 2026.
**Moved here from the Scope Questionnaire:** old 4.1 names, 4.2 Bloodveil's faction name, 4.3 Afflicted fantasy, 4.4 Militia identity, 4.5 Templars direction, 4.10 Smash & Bash silhouette, 16.8 portrait style.

> **Updated 3 November 2026 — city-builder direction.** The game is now a city-builder, not an RTS. Five teams are playable: **Survivors, The Hunters, Militia, Ashen, Droids**. **Scientists, Afflicted, Mutants and The Pack** are now *Warlords of the Wastes* — NPC factions you meet and trade with; they still need names, faces and a personality for their info card, so their questions stay. **Bloodveil, Templars and Nomads are cut** — their questions are marked *CUT* and can be skipped. Combat is gone for now, so read "fight", "weapon" or "signature unit" below as costume and character, not mechanics. See `Pages of Eden/Data/Visions_of_Eden.md`.

**How to answer.** Under each question write `A`, `A but with …`, or your own line. A blank is "not decided". A suggestion is an offer, not a decision.

**Filling a lot at once.** In section 3 you can write `ALL A` and then only note the portraits you want changed.

**Handing this to another model.** Paste the file and add: "Fill only the Answer lines. Treat every option as an offer. Where I have written an answer, obey it. Where I have not, take the option marked suggested and say which letter you used. Do not change Viktor and Ayla's likeness. Do not write image prompts yet."

Colours below are already in the codex. Approving the hex values is still Scope Questionnaire 16.4. Whether we hire voice actors is still 17.2 — a line of dialogue here is character, not a recording plan.

## 0. The picture style

Viktor.jpg and Ayla.jpg are painted, half-body, three-quarter, on a flat grey studio ground. Cloth is worn and mended. Leather is dull. Dirt is in the seams. One accent colour lives in the clothing, not in a glow. Nobody is mid-slogan. Ayla is guarded, not posed. Viktor is tired, not snarling.
